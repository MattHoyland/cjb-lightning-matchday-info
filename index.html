Help and support

CJB Lightning MatchDay supports the CJB Lightning football team community. For help, contact Matt Hoyland at mhoyland@btopenworld.com.

Signing in

Create an account with the email you gave your coach. Enter the verification code from your email in the app. A coach must link your account to your child before family information is available.

Report a problem

Include your phone model, app version and what you were doing. Do not send passwords, verification codes, access tokens or private information about other families.

Delete your account

Open Account → Delete account in the app. Enter your current password and type DELETE, then confirm. The only owner of a team must appoint another owner first. Upload pending match changes before deleting. See the privacy policy for what is removed and what shared team information remains. Contact Matt if you cannot access the app or want to discuss a child's team records.
